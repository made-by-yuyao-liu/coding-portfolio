The distribution of Canadian hockey players reveals a detailed gender profile across the provinces, highlighting differences in regional engagement. Data shows Ontario leading with the highest number of players, signaling a strong hockey culture. Possible reasons for the higher proportion of hockey players from Ontario is its large population compared to other provinces, better funding for sports, as well as people's higher income levels, might make it more feasible for individuals in Ontario to pursue hockey.

Examining hockey player demographics across U.S. states reveals a balanced male-to-female ratio. The data highlights Minnesota and Massachusetts as having a substantially higher representation of hockey players. Reasons for this could be the cold winters in both states, which facilitates outdoor ice sports like hockey. Minnesota, in particular, is known as the "State of Hockey" as its climate supports ice hockey for a significant part of the year.
