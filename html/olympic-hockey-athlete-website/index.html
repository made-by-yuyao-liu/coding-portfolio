Age

Data about athletes is collected when they register for the Olympic Games. One of the categories of the data collected is age.
It can have a significant impact on athlete performance because of its direct relationship to physical and mental capabilities.
Maybe younger athletes may benefit from enhanced physical attributes such as speed, agility, and flexibility.
However, older athletes may possess greater experience, tactical awareness, and mental resilience, which can compensate for any decline in physical abilities.

Age Comparison in the Canadian Team

The range of age goes from 31 to 43 years old for the men in Team Canada. And it is from 28 to 39 years old for the women of this team.


Age Comparison in the American Team

The range of age goes from 26 to 45 years old for the men in Team USA. And it is from 25 to 37 years old for the women of this team.
